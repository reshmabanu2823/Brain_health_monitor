Calm Index

Relative Alpha (8–13 Hz)
— %
Experimental Awaiting
Formula: (Relative Alpha / 0.45) × 100%

Derived from relative alpha spectral power bounded between 0% and 100%. Research indicator only; does not establish clinical relaxation or emotional wellbeing.

Focus Index

Beta / (Theta + Alpha)
— ratio
Experimental Awaiting
Formula: Beta / (Theta + Alpha)

Ratio of high-frequency beta power to slow-to-mid rhythms. Used in experimental neurofeedback paradigms. Does not prove cognitive concentration.

Mental Workload

Theta / Alpha Ratio
— ratio
Exploratory Awaiting
Formula: Theta Power / Alpha Power

Dimensionless ratio reflecting frontocentral theta dynamics relative to parietal alpha synchronization. Sensitive to task demands and electrode placement.

Fatigue / Drowsiness

(Theta + Alpha) / Beta
— ratio
Exploratory Awaiting
Formula: (Theta + Alpha) / Beta

Reflects proportional slowing of background activity. Single-channel estimates can be swayed by blink transients, movement, and electrode drift.

Standard electrophysiological research metric (TBR). Highly influenced by channel montage and subject age; explicitly NOT an ADHD diagnostic instrument.
