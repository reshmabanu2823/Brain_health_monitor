EEG Classifier Feasibility & Model Explanations

Zero Fabricated Predictions

Model Installation Status: No trained EEG classifier model is installed. In accordance with research integrity standards, CORTIA does not output fabricated stress scores, depression indices, or attention percentages.

Why are psychiatric and sleep models withheld on this hardware?
  • Sleep Architecture Staging: Mandates multi-channel polysomnography (C3/C4 central EEG for sleep spindles and K-complexes, dual EOG for rapid eye movements, and submental chin EMG for muscle atonia). Single-channel uncalibrated forehead telemetry cannot resolve sleep stages.
  • Depression Screening (FAA): Frontal Alpha Asymmetry mathematically requires a homologous bilateral electrode pair (ln[F4] - ln[F3]). A single bipolar biopotential channel cannot measure inter-hemispheric differential power.
  • Anxiety State Classification: Single-channel forehead biopotentials are contaminated by frontal electromyographic tension, requiring a 10-20 montage and normative referencing.
